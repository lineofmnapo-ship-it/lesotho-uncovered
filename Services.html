Rock Art & Prehistoric Sites 09:00am-17:00pm Dinosaur Footprints

Booking Process: No advance booking platform exists for the raw site itself. A small sign marks the turn-off from the main road. You simply arrive during daylight hours, and an on-site community guide will meet you to conduct the tour.

Logistics & Best Time to Visit: The conservation fee is paid directly in cash to the community guide on-site. Crucial Tip: The footprints sit in a flat sandstone riverbed. If the river is in high flood after seasonal rains, the footprints will be submerged and hidden from view. Visit during the lower-water dry seasons or clear weather spells.
Kome Cave Dwellings home Tour 09:00am-17:00pm Kome Cave Dwellings

There are no formal overnight accommodations inside the caves to respect the privacy of the resident families; it is strictly a day-trip destination.

Operating Hours: The Visitor Information Center runs from 08:00 AM to 05:00 PM daily. Visitors should arrive before 02:00 PM to allow time for the hike down and back up before closing.

Booking Process: Entry fees are paid directly to the local community at the information desk upon arrival. For group packages, multi-stop itineraries, or pre-arranged transport from Maseru, bookings can be funneled through regional operators.
